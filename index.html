Six legends. Five levels. One relentless Swimsuit Willem.

SELECT YOUR CHARACTER

All six heroes are unlocked. They play the same, but each has their own style.

Move with arrow keys / WASD or the touch buttons. Avoid Swimsuit Willem! Collect every glowing relic, then reach the golden exit. In the boss arena, break the seals by approaching the boss after collecting the relics.
